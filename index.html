05 / 10
AI philosophy

AI is a copilot. The student is the pilot.

Where AI is used

  • Natural-language study search with cited sources — not anonymous paragraphs.
  • Cross-tab synthesis: summarize, compare, and build tables from what is already open.
  • Always user-initiated. The copilot does not run in the background on private notes.

Where AI is not used

  • Timers, Eisenhower matrix, streaks, and local notes work with the model off.
  • No silent upload of student PII. No "do my homework" mode.
  • Core product remains useful on a dead network — that is the point.

This is how we reduce AI dependence, rather than increase it: one cited copilot instead of ten chat windows, and a study OS that still works when the model is down. Development use of AI is documented in the public repository. Architecture and product decisions are the team's.
